LIMITATIONS THAT BUILD TRUST

What this experiment cannot tell you

Small sample

Only 299 records are available, with 60 in the held-out test set.

Threshold trade-off

Lowering the cutoff can find more events, but may create more false alarms.

Sampling uncertainty

A single split can change observed test metrics; five-fold CV was used for tuning, not external validation.

Prediction ≠ diagnosis

Associations learned from retrospective records do not establish causes or medical recommendations.

Follow-up time

The `time` feature is part of the supplied data and may contain information unavailable in a true early-warning setting.

Generalization

The UCI cohort may not represent current hospitals, populations, or treatment standards.
